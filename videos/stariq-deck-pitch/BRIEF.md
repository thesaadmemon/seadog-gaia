# StarIQ — narrated investor deck

## Intent

The 12-slide "StarIQ — Investor Deck" turned into a 3–4 minute narrated video, same narrator as the
orbital pitch. Simplicity is the key: one idea per slide, large type, one hero image each, quiet motion.

## Source rules

- Every figure, claim and footnote comes from the deck and its speaker notes.
- Caveats from the notes are kept: filings are applications, not deployments; the 99.6 % paper is under
  review and the protected grid is an illustrative re-render; the title satellite is a rendering; target
  buyers are not customers yet; co-founder names are under NDA.
- Left out for simplicity: the proposed (unconfirmed) CFO, the €1.1M EDGX reference, the €90k module line.

## Assets

- Deck photos and marks (beam-test rig, chips on the beam, Pi cluster, Perseverance, Ingenuity, title render,
  layer render, inference grids, logos, founder portrait).
- Orbital b-roll clips cut from the first video's three.js render (constellation, closing sunrise).
- Voice: local Kokoro-82M `af_heart`. Score: `tools/score.py` (calm variant). SFX: Pixabay-licensed bundle.
